    Four separate judgments

    Rate the specific topic, depth, video quality, and broader subject independently. You can leave any field empty. Medium interest means indifference. Medium quality means acceptable quality.

    Check the AI topic label. Correct it when needed. Video level describes the material; depth describes how it fits you. A video can be good and too basic.

    Watch at your pace

    Opening a video does not mark it watched. Unwatched videos compete with new candidates in the next inspection pool. Dismiss removes a video from that pool without creating a preference rating. Restore reverses dismissal.

    Choose your sources
